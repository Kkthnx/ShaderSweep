Restart your PC?

Save your work first. ShaderSweep never closes your apps for you.

  • Save your documents, projects and game progress.
  • Close games and anything that is recording, rendering or downloading.
  • If an app has unsaved changes, Windows asks you about it and waits for you.
  • After you press Restart you get a countdown, and you can cancel until it ends.

Use Restart rather than Shut down. Windows only finishes removing queued files on a full restart.
